Preview limitations
Canvas anchors, offsets, alignment and box padding are read from slots. Image brush sizes set texture bounds; exported UV regions crop the PNG images. Missing inherited defaults use approximate browser values. Custom widgets are labelled placeholders. Materials, transforms and non-default clipping are not reproduced. PNGs are extracted from editor Texture2D source data; they do not reproduce runtime compression, material effects or texture streaming.
